Foxiem is a simple counter for habits, goals, reps and everyday counts. Open it, tap, and keep going — without an account, and without a complicated setup.

Create named trackers for different things you care about. Count quickly with +1, +5 or −1, add a note when you need context, and pick up where you left off.

See how the numbers move. History, insights and consistency views help you notice patterns over days and weeks, not just the current total.

Set reminders when you want a nudge. Android widgets let you count from the home screen when that extra step is useful.

Foxiem is local-first. Your trackers, counts and notes live on the device. You can use the app as a guest. Signing in is optional and does not replace the counters already on your phone.

Optional Foxiem Pro unlocks extra insights, reminder options, widgets and related tools. Monthly, yearly and lifetime purchases are optional. Restore purchases if you already bought Pro.

Purchases, optional sign-in, crash reports and opt-in analytics are described in the privacy policy.
